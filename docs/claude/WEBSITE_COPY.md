# Brain Game: A Study in Cerebral Geography

**Learn how your brain works by playing with it.**

Brain Game drops you in front of a 3D brain rendered in the style of a Victorian woodblock engraving. Words fall from above. "SPEECH." "PLANNING A TRIP." "PHANTOM LIMB SENSATION." Your job: rotate the brain and catch each word on the region responsible for that function.

Get it right and the region illuminates. Get it wrong and the word shatters, but the correct answer reveals itself. Either way, you learn.

No flashcards. No multiple choice. You build spatial memory by physically manipulating a brain, the same kind of motor-driven learning the game teaches you about.

Four difficulty tiers take you from the basics (vision lives in the occipital lobe) to the edge of current neuroscience (why you feel your phone vibrate when it didn't). Every answer is traceable to peer-reviewed research. Every region mapping honors the brain's real complexity, because when a function involves multiple regions, we don't pretend it doesn't.

Built with React, Three.js, and custom GLSL shaders. Free to play in your browser.
