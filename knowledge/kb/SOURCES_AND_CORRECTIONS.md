## Source hierarchy

1. Canon: `brain_regions.json` (game regions only).
2. Authority: Kandel, Principles of Neural Science; Purves, Neuroscience; Nolte, The Human Brain; peer-reviewed papers named inline.

4. Never primary: pop-science books, news briefs, single-study press releases.

## Independent fact-check, 2026-10-02

A separate reviewer checked all 20 content files. 8 errors fixed: cerebellar granule cells are mostly born after birth; operculated sulcus definition; BBB history (Stern named it ~1921, Lewandowsky proposed, Goldmann 1909 and 1913); cerebellar outputs "main" not "only"; vestibulocerebellum is the flocculonodular lobe only; meningioma, not glioma, is the most common brain tumour; Boldrini vs Moreno-Jiménez attribution; MS causes myelin loss, not the reverse. About 20 softer wording fixes applied (hedges, interneuron definition, Dale's principle, pineal pronunciation, grey/white ratio about 55/45).
