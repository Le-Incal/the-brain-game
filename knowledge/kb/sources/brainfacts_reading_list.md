## Reuse rules (read first)

- BrainFacts is run by the Society for Neuroscience (SfN). Their Reprints and Permissions page requires **written permission for any reuse** of wording, images or video. No open license.
  https://www.brainfacts.org/archives/2012/reprints-and-permissions
- robots.txt blocks automated fetching of several pages (terms of use, Core Concepts, 3D Brain, the book page). **Do not scrape.** Read by hand.
- Our route: paraphrase facts in our own words and cite. Facts are not copyrightable; wording is.
- Some pages are not SfN's own: `/archives/2012/` items are from CIHR's "The Brain from Top to Bottom", and the Neurological Disorders A-Z is copied from NINDS. Cite the original publisher.
- Free book: **Brain Facts, 8th edition (2018)**. https://www.brainfacts.org/the-brain-facts-book. Most "Brain Primer" articles are adapted from it and were re-reviewed 2019 to 2023. Highest-value set.

## Flags to carry into writing

- **Smell and the thalamus:** "Making Sense of Scents" says all smell goes through the thalamus. Consensus: olfactory bulb projects directly to piriform cortex, bypassing the thalamus. Our forebrain file already states the consensus.
- **"Trillion odors"** (2014) is disputed. Do not use.
- **The Brain Divide (2024)** half-reinforces left-analytical / right-creative while debunking it. Paraphrase carefully.
- **Phineas Gage:** BrainFacts pieces omit his documented social recovery (Macmillan). We tell the full story.
- **Prefrontal maturity age:** site says both late 20s and early 30s. Proposal: "keeps maturing into the mid-to-late twenties" and avoid a single number.
- **Alzheimer's (2020)** predates lecanemab (2023) and donanemab (2024). **Glioma (2019)** predates WHO CNS5 (2021).
- **Bright Light Neuro Lesson 1** uses 100 billion neurons. Copy tone only.

- Networks: no salience or central executive network.
- History: nothing on Galen, Vesalius, Willis, Brodmann, or Wernicke himself.

- Deep structures: no insula or thalamus articles.
